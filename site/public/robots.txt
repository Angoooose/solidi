# Filters, sorts and "Show more" (?denomination=, ?material=, ?limit=, …) multiply into endless
# views of the same page. Each is noindex (lib/seo.ts), but crawling them runs the page's facet
# queries against D1 on every combination, past the edge cache. Only the collection-scoped index
# pages (/people?collection=roman-imperial) are pages of their own; the longer Allow wins over
# Disallow: /*?, and the longer Disallow beats the Allow again once anything follows the collection.
Disallow: /*?
Allow: /people?collection=
Allow: /mints?collection=
Allow: /denominations?collection=
Allow: /subjects?collection=
Disallow: /people?collection=*&
Disallow: /mints?collection=*&
Disallow: /denominations?collection=*&
Disallow: /subjects?collection=*&
